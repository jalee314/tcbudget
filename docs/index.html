Track your Pokémon collection like a portfolio

TCBudget helps collectors manage inventory, sealed products, pack openings, and profit & loss — with local data and optional live market prices.

Built for serious collectors

  • Portfolio P&L

    Cost basis, market value, realized and unrealized gains in one grid.

  • Sealed & opened tracking

    Log boxes and packs, open sealed inventory, and tie pulls back to products.

  • Pack-opening experience

    Animated reveals when you rip packs — fun without losing the numbers.

  • Analytics

    Charts and breakdowns to see how your collection performs over time.

  • Market watchlist

    Track cards you care about (JustTCG API key optional for live prices).

  • Private by default

    SQLite database on your machine — no account required to use the app.
